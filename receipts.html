R-01 · Nursing Board probation

Tempe BSN program, 36-month probation

Arizona State Board of Nursing, In the Matter of Arizona College BSN Program, Tempe Campus, Consent Agreement for Probation & Order No. 210504RN96500100, effective May 2022. Findings of Fact ¶2: Board received and identified 14 complaints between May 6 and November 3, 2021. Order: program approval placed on probation for a minimum of 36 months; early termination eligible at 30 months. AZBN public list later shows “Arizona College (Tempe) — Prior Board Action — 5/25/22 – 2/24/25” with current full approval.

PDF circulating via Spectrum News: Consent Agreement (arizona college). AZBN violations list: azbn.gov education program lists.

Disposition: Institutional consent order. Not a personal nursing-license action against Nick Mansour. He was Chairman/CEO per his own bio at the time the complaints accrued and the order issued.
